# Boss Rocky — integrated animation assets

`boss-rocky-idle-master-v1.png` is the initial review candidate, generated with built-in imagegen. Original identity reference: symbol 883 frame 18, exported at 6× in `docs/boss-rocky-original/`. Approved standard pigeon v2 supplied linework/shading style only. Exact prompt: `concept-prompt.txt`.

The design was approved. Eight states are integrated: idle, fly, hop, landing, dropping attack, hit, death and rock throw. The approved master supplies neutral idle; seven action sheets were generated with built-in imagegen. Exact prompts: `animation-prompts.json`. The throw sheet also uses original frame 49 as a motion reference; it preserves alternating wing sweeps rather than adding human hands or a held rock.

Original symbol 883 timing at 30 FPS: idle 2 frames, fly 8, hop 9, landing 16, dropping attack 9, hit 13, death 11, rock throw 11. Six illustrated keys are held on each original action range. The original animation player still runs transitions/events. Rock-throw weapon activation and projectile generation remain unchanged, including their original fire rate. The original 6px animation offset on death/throw is retained in presentation. AI, health (20), resistance (15), collision, rewards and effects remain unchanged.

Original idle alpha bounds at 6×: 279 × 416, approximately 46.5 × 69.3 logical pixels. Prepared art matches the original height uniformly, keeping its approved aspect ratio. The 90 × 90 capture uses 270 × 270 cells at one-third rendering scale and local foot anchor y=35. `tools/prepare-boss-rocky-assets.ps1` writes 48 prepared keys and `boss-rocky-atlas.png` (1620 × 2160). The renderer loads Rocky's embedded atlas separately from the regular pigeon atlas; both remain shared across instances.

Rebuild with the preparation script, then `node tools/build-remaster.cjs`. Review all states at `/boss-rocky-preview`. `node tools/make-boss-rocky-qa.cjs` creates a separate `/boss-rocky-qa` test movie that replaces Day 1 XML with one BossRockyPigeon and two standard pigeons for comparison. Release movie/campaign data are unchanged; select Day 1 in the test movie.

Validation: build and `git diff --check` passed. All eight states were inspected in the atlas/gallery with transparent backgrounds. The test movie showed Rocky and standard pigeons together at their different sizes, on/above the roof, with projectiles, player damage and scoring visible. Current-load logs contained no AVM or sprite-loading errors. Evidence: `docs/boss-rocky-animation-review.jpg` and `docs/boss-rocky-gameplay.jpg`. Rock-throw pose timing was reviewed in the gallery and is wired to the existing weapon event; an individual live rock-throw release was not separately captured. Full boss defeat, reward and campaign progression were not tested in this pass.
