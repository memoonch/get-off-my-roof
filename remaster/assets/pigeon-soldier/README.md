# Soldier pigeon — integrated animations

`soldier-pigeon-idle-master-v1.png` is the first high-quality design candidate, generated with built-in imagegen. Reference: original symbol 976 frame 19, exported at 6× in `docs/soldier-pigeon-original/`. The approved standard pigeon v2 supplied rendering style only. Exact prompt: `concept-prompt.txt`.

The design was approved and seven states are integrated for SoldierPigeon, BlackSoldierPigeon and BrownSoldierPigeon. The two-frame idle holds the approved master; six action sheets were generated using built-in imagegen. Exact prompts: `animation-prompts.json`. The death cycle omits source key 5 because it repeats the earlier tilt, returning from key 4 to key 0 to complete the tumble.

Original symbol 976 timing at 30 FPS: idle 2 frames, flight 8, hop 10, landing 19, attack 12, hit 14, death 11. The animation player still controls original transitions and events; frame maps hold the illustrated keys across these exact counts. Original idle alpha bounds at 6×: 200 × 374, approximately 33.3 × 62.3 logical pixels. Prepared art matches the original height uniformly and retains the approved aspect ratio. Foot anchor is local y=30. The 70 × 70 capture is padded into shared 90 × 80 cells at 3× resolution.

Preparation: `tools/prepare-soldier-pigeon-assets.ps1`, then `node tools/build-remaster.cjs`. Prerequisites are the prepared standard/fat atlases. Outputs: `pigeon-atlas.png`, `frames/`, `animation-manifest.json`, and `combined-pigeon-atlas.png`. The combined atlas uses 12 columns × 11 rows (3240 × 2640), 126 keys for standard, fat and soldier families; previous art retains its registration. Gray/black/brown use the original color offsets. Other families and bosses retain original art. AI, health, projectile effects and collisions are unchanged.

Review at `/soldier-pigeon-preview`. `node tools/make-soldier-pigeon-qa.cjs` creates a separate `/soldier-pigeon-qa` movie with all three soldier variants in Day 1 for testing while Day 11 is locked. It replaces only that test movie's Day 1 XML; the release SWF and campaign progression are untouched.
